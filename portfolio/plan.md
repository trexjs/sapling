# Marketing Site Plan for Schools

Based on the data structure, here's a comprehensive list of pages for a school marketing website:

## Core Pages (Essential)

- **Homepage** - Hero section with school logo, rating (3.5/5), key statistics (student count, teacher strength), quick highlights, and CTA for admissions
- **About Us** - Year of establishment, foundation statement from Principal, school values, and mission
- **Academics** - Detailed courses (K10: ICSE/CBSE/State Board, K12: Science/Commerce/Language streams), eligibility criteria, class structure
- **Admissions** - Application process, eligibility requirements, important dates, enrollment forms, and contact information
- **Contact Us** - Full address with map (lat/long), phone numbers, email, social media links, inquiry form

## Facilities & Infrastructure

- **Campus & Facilities** - Photo gallery, infrastructure details (transportation, CCTV, fire safety, drinking water, dining hall, bookstore, smart classes, AC classrooms, internet-enabled campus)
- **Boarding Facilities** - Hostel information (boys/girls), food menu (veg/non-veg), laundry services (conditional page based on school_type)

## Academic Excellence

- **Achievements & Events** - Recent achievements with photos/videos, event calendar (hackathons, essay competitions, quizzes, sports day, olympiads, best idea competitions)
- **Alumni** - Notable alumni profiles with their academic journey and success stories
- **Reviews & Testimonials** - Public reviews from Google and other platforms, ratings, parent feedback

## Student Development

- **Skill Development Programs** - Extracurricular offerings (yoga, music, swimming, chess, karate, coding, public speaking, summer camps, foreign languages)
- **Scholarships & Financial Aid** - Available scholarship programs (government, NGOs, private corporations, HNI, fundraisals, adoption programs), value-added services (loans, emergency cash)

## Resources

- **Downloads** - Brochures, foundation statements, academic calendars, important forms and documents

## Optional/Utility Pages

- **Virtual Tour** - 360° campus walkthrough using school images
- **News & Updates** - Latest announcements and school news
- **Career Opportunities** - Job openings for teachers and staff
- **FAQ** - Common questions about admissions, facilities, and academics
